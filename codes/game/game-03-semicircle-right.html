📘 基本定义Definition

顶点在圆上，两边与圆相交的角，叫做圆周角。

An inscribed angle is an angle formed by two chords in a circle with the vertex on the circle.

📐 定理内容Theorem

在任意一个圆中：

In any circle:

  • 半圆（直径）所对的圆周角一定是直角
  • The inscribed angle of a semicircle is always a right angle
  • 直径所对的圆周角恒等于 90°
  • The inscribed angle subtended by a diameter is always 90°

📌 重要推论Corollary

如果三角形的一条边是圆的直径，且第三个顶点在圆上，该三角形一定是直角三角形。

If one side of a triangle is the diameter of a circle and the third vertex lies on the circle, the triangle must be a right triangle.
