07 - 垂径定理 07 - Perpendicular Chord Bisector Theorem

📘 基本定义📘 Definition

垂直于弦的直径平分弦，并且平分弦所对的两条弧，这条直径所在的直线叫做圆的垂径。

The diameter perpendicular to a chord bisects the chord and the arcs subtended by the chord. This diameter is called the perpendicular chord bisector of the circle.

📐 定理内容📐 Theorem

在任意一个圆中：

In any circle:

  • 垂直于弦的直径平分这条弦，并且平分弦所对的两条弧。
  • The diameter perpendicular to a chord bisects the chord and the two arcs subtended by the chord.

📌 重要推论📌 Corollary

1. 平分弦（不是直径）的直径垂直于弦，并且平分弦所对的两条弧；

1. The diameter that bisects a chord (not a diameter) is perpendicular to the chord and bisects the arcs subtended by the chord;

2. 弦的垂直平分线经过圆心，并且平分弦所对的两条弧。

2. The perpendicular bisector of a chord passes through the center and bisects the arcs subtended by the chord.
