02 - 同弧圆周角定理 02 - Inscribed Angle Theorem

📘 基本定义 📘 Definition

顶点在圆上，两边与圆相交的角，叫做圆周角。

An inscribed angle is an angle formed by two chords in a circle with the vertex on the circle.

📐 定理内容 📐 Theorem

在同圆或等圆中：

In the same or congruent circles:

  • 同弧所对的圆周角相等
  • Inscribed angles subtended by the same arc are equal
  • 圆周角 = 所对弧的圆心角的一半
  • An inscribed angle is half its central angle

📌 重要推论 📌 Corollary

半圆所对的圆周角是直角；直径所对的圆周角等于 90°。

An angle inscribed in a semicircle is a right angle (90°).
