05 - Tangent Perpendicular to Radius Theorem

📘 Basic Definition

A tangent to a circle is a line that touches the circle at exactly one point (the point of tangency).

📐 Theorem Content

The radius drawn to the point of tangency is perpendicular to the tangent line.

Conversely, if a line is perpendicular to a radius at its endpoint on the circle, then the line is a tangent.

📌 Important Corollary

• The tangent line and radius form a 90° angle at the point of contact.

• From any external point, two tangents to a circle are equal in length.

Key Insight: The tangent line is always perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.

🛠️ Interactive Simulation

∠OAP: 90°
Status: Perpendicular
Example 1: If OA is a radius and PA is tangent at A, then ∠OAP = 90°.

🌍 Real-World Application

Used in optics (lens design), engineering (wheel alignment), and navigation (determining tangential paths).

📜 Historical Context

First proved by Euclid in "Elements" (Book III, Proposition 18). This theorem is fundamental to circle geometry.

❓ Practice Problems

1. If the radius is 5 cm and the tangent line makes a 90° angle with the radius, what is the distance from the center to the tangent line?

2. From point P outside a circle, two tangents PA and PB are drawn. If PA = 7 cm, what is PB?

3. Prove that the tangent at any point on a circle is perpendicular to the radius at that point.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

• Confusing tangent with secant (secant intersects at two points)

• Forgetting the right angle at the point of tangency

• Assuming the tangent can intersect the circle at more than one point

🔍 Interactive Challenge

Drag point A around the circle to see how the tangent line and radius maintain their perpendicular relationship.

Hint: The angle between the radius and tangent is always 90° at the point of contact.

Note: This theorem is the basis for many other circle theorems and applications in physics.

05 - 切线半径垂直定理 05 - Tangent Perpendicular to Radius Theorem

📘 基本定义Definition

圆的切线是指与圆只有唯一公共点的直线，该公共点称为切点。

A tangent to a circle is a line that intersects the circle at exactly one point, called the point of tangency.

📐 定理内容Theorem

在任意一个圆中：

In any circle:

  • 圆的切线垂直于过切点的半径。
  • The tangent to a circle is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency.

📌 重要推论Corollary

经过圆心且垂直于切线的直线必经过切点；经过切点且垂直于切线的直线必经过圆心。

The line perpendicular to the tangent at the point of contact passes through the center of the circle.
