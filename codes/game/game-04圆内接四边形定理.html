04 - Cyclic Quadrilateral Theorem

📘 Basic Definition

A cyclic quadrilateral is a quadrilateral whose vertices all lie on a single circle.

📐 Theorem Content

In a cyclic quadrilateral:

• Opposite angles are supplementary (sum to 180°)

• An exterior angle is equal to the interior opposite angle

📌 Important Corollary

• If a quadrilateral has supplementary opposite angles, it is cyclic.

• The sum of all interior angles is 360° (as in any quadrilateral).

Key Insight: ∠A + ∠C = 180° and ∠B + ∠D = 180° in cyclic quadrilateral ABCD.

🛠️ Interactive Simulation

∠A: 0°
∠B: 0°
∠C: 0°
∠D: 0°
Example 1: If ∠A = 110°, then ∠C = 70° in a cyclic quadrilateral.

🌍 Real-World Application

Used in mechanical engineering for designing gear systems and in architecture for creating symmetrical circular structures with quadrilateral elements.

📜 Historical Context

Known to ancient Greek mathematicians. The theorem was fully developed in the 17th century by mathematicians studying circle theorems.

❓ Practice Problems

1. In cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, ∠A = 120°. What is ∠C?

2. If ∠B = 75° and ∠D = 105°, is ABCD cyclic?

3. Prove that a rectangle is always a cyclic quadrilateral.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

• Forgetting that the theorem only applies to quadrilaterals with all vertices on a circle

• Confusing supplementary angles with equal angles

• Assuming all quadrilaterals are cyclic (only specific ones are)

🔍 Interactive Challenge

Drag points A, B, C, D to see how the angles change while maintaining the cyclic property.

Hint: The sum of opposite angles should always be 180° in a cyclic quadrilateral.

Note: This theorem has many applications in advanced geometry and trigonometry.

04 - 圆内接四边形定理 04 - Cyclic Quadrilateral Theorem

📘 基本定义Definition

四个顶点都在同一个圆上，且按顺时针依次为 A、B、C、D 的四边形，叫做圆内接四边形。

A quadrilateral with vertices A, B, C, D in clockwise order on a circle is called a cyclic quadrilateral.

📐 定理内容Theorem

在任意一个圆中：

In any circle:

  • 圆内接四边形的对角互补，即对角之和等于 180°
  • The opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary, summing to 180°.

📌 重要推论Corollary

如果一个四边形的一组对角互补，那么这个四边形内接于一个圆。

If a pair of opposite angles of a quadrilateral are supplementary, the quadrilateral is cyclic.

∠ABC：0°
∠BCD：0°
∠CDA：0°
∠DAB：0°

∠DAB + ∠BCD = 0°
∠ABC + ∠CDA = 0°
