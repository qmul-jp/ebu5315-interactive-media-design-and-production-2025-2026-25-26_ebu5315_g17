06 - Tangent Length Theorem

📘 Basic Definition

Two tangent segments drawn from the same external point to a circle are equal in length.

📐 Theorem Content

For any external point P and a circle with center O:

• If PA and PB are tangents to the circle (A and B are points of tangency), then PA = PB.

• The line OP bisects ∠APB and ∠AOB.

📌 Important Corollary

• The two tangent segments from a common external point are congruent.

• The angles between the tangents and the line to the center are equal.

Key Insight: PA = PB for any external point P.

🌍 Real-World Application

Used in GPS navigation systems to calculate distances from satellites to Earth's surface, and in robotics for path planning around circular obstacles.

📜 Historical Context

Formalized by Euclid in "Elements" (Book III, Proposition 36). This theorem is fundamental in circle geometry and trigonometry.

❓ Practice Problems

1. From point P outside a circle, two tangents PA and PB are drawn. If PA = 12 cm, what is PB?

2. The distance from P to the center O is 15 cm, and the radius is 9 cm. Calculate the tangent length.

3. Prove that PA = PB using the Pythagorean Theorem.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

• Confusing tangent segments with secant segments

• Assuming the theorem applies to chords (it only applies to tangents)

• Forgetting that the external point must be outside the circle

06 - 切线长定理 06 - Tangent Length Theorem

📘 基本定义📘 Definition

从圆外一点向圆作切线，该点与切点之间的线段长度，叫做这点到圆的切线长。

The length of the segment from an external point to the point of tangency is called the tangent length from the point to the circle.

📐 定理内容📐 Theorem

在任意一个圆中：

  • 从圆外一点引圆的两条切线，它们的切线长相等。
  • The two tangent segments from a single external point to a circle are equal in length.

📌 重要推论📌 Corollary

圆心与圆外一点的连线，平分这一点所引的两条切线的夹角，并且垂直平分两条切点的连线。

The line from the center to an external point bisects the angle between the two tangents and is the perpendicular bisector of the chord joining the points of contact.

PA = PB = 0.000
