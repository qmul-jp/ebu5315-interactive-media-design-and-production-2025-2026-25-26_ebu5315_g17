03 - Semicircle Right Angle Theorem

📘 Basic Definition

An inscribed angle that intercepts a semicircle is a right angle (90°).

📐 Theorem Content

If an inscribed angle intercepts a semicircle (180° arc), then the angle is 90°.

Conversely, if an inscribed angle is 90°, it intercepts a semicircle.

📌 Important Corollary

This is a special case of the Inscribed Angle Theorem (½ × 180° = 90°).

Key Insight: Any triangle inscribed in a semicircle is a right triangle.

🛠️ Interactive Simulation

∠ABC: 0°
弧AC: 0°
Example 1: In a semicircle, any inscribed angle will always be 90°.

🌍 Real-World Application

Used in construction to ensure right angles when laying out circular foundations or in the design of circular arches and bridges.

📜 Historical Context

Known to ancient Greek mathematicians as a consequence of the Inscribed Angle Theorem. Mentioned in Euclid's Elements (Book III, Proposition 31).

❓ Practice Problems

1. If ∠ABC is inscribed in a semicircle, what is its measure?

2. A triangle inscribed in a circle has a 90° angle. What can you say about the side opposite this angle?

3. In a circle with diameter AB, point C is on the circle. If ∠ACB = 90°, what is the measure of arc AB?

⚠️ Common Mistakes

• Assuming all right angles in circles are from semicircles (not true for all right angles)

• Confusing this with the Central Angle Theorem

• Forgetting the diameter must be the hypotenuse of the right triangle

Hint: The angle is 90° only when the intercepted arc is exactly 180° (a semicircle).

03 - 半圆直角定理 03 - Semicircle Right Angle Theorem

📘 基本定义📘 Definition

顶点在圆上，两边与圆相交的角，叫做圆周角。 An inscribed angle is an angle formed by two chords in a circle with the vertex on the circle.

📐 定理内容📐 Theorem

在任意一个圆中：In any circle:

  • 半圆（直径）所对的圆周角一定是直角The inscribed angle of a semicircle is always a right angle
  • 直径所对的圆周角恒等于 90°The inscribed angle subtended by a diameter is always 90°

📌 重要推论📌 Corollary

如果三角形的一条边是圆的直径，且第三个顶点在圆上，该三角形一定是直角三角形。 If one side of a triangle is the diameter of a circle and the third vertex lies on the circle, the triangle must be a right triangle.
