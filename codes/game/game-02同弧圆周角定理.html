02 - Inscribed Angle Theorem

📘 Basic Definition

An inscribed angle has its vertex on the circle and sides that are chords of the circle.

📐 Theorem Content

In the same circle (or congruent circles):

The measure of an inscribed angle is half the measure of its intercepted arc.

Equal inscribed angles ⇔ Equal intercepted arcs

📌 Important Corollary

• Inscribed angles subtended by the same arc are equal.

• An angle inscribed in a semicircle is a right angle.

Key Insight: Inscribed angle = ½ × Intercepted arc

🛠️ Interactive Simulation

∠ABC: 0°
弧AC: 0°
Example 1: If arc AC = 120°, then ∠ABC = 60°.

🌍 Real-World Application

Architects use this theorem when designing circular windows and domes to ensure symmetry and structural integrity.

📜 Historical Context

Formalized by Ptolemy in the 2nd century CE. This theorem is crucial for trigonometric calculations in ancient astronomy.

❓ Practice Problems

1. If an inscribed angle measures 35°, what is the measure of its intercepted arc?

2. Two inscribed angles intercept the same arc. If one measures 45°, what is the other?

3. In a circle, an inscribed angle intercepts a 180° arc. What is the angle measure?

⚠️ Common Mistakes

• Forgetting the ½ factor in the theorem

• Confusing inscribed angles with central angles

• Misidentifying the intercepted arc (must be the arc opposite the angle)

02 - 同弧圆周角定理 02 - Inscribed Angle Theorem

📘 基本定义📘 Definition

顶点在圆上，两边与圆相交的角，叫做圆周角。An inscribed angle is an angle formed by two chords in a circle with the vertex on the circle.

📐 定理内容📐 Theorem

在同圆或等圆中：In the same or congruent circles:

  • 同弧所对的圆周角相等Inscribed angles subtended by the same arc are equal
  • 圆周角 = 所对弧的圆心角的一半An inscribed angle is half its central angle

📌 重要推论📌 Corollary

半圆所对的圆周角是直角；直径所对的圆周角等于 90°。 An angle inscribed in a semicircle is a right angle (90°).

∠ACB (圆周角)：0°
弧 AB：0
