08 - 弦切角定理 08 - Tangent-Chord Angle Theorem

📘 基本定义📘 Definition

顶点在圆上，一边与圆相切，另一边与圆相交的角叫做弦切角。

An angle with its vertex on a circle, one side tangent to the circle and the other side intersecting the circle is called a tangent-chord angle.

📐 定理内容📐 Theorem

在任意一个圆中：

In any circle:

  • 弦切角等于它所夹的弧所对的圆周角。
  • The tangent-chord angle is equal to the inscribed angle subtended by the intercepted arc.

📌 重要推论📌 Corollary

1. 弦切角的度数等于它所夹弧的度数的一半；

1. The measure of a tangent-chord angle equals half the measure of its intercepted arc;

2. 同弧或等弧所对的弦切角相等。

2. Tangent-chord angles subtended by the same or equal arcs are equal.
